# Fabián R. Vega

Historian working on books, libraries, and religious texts in the early modern Iberian world, with digital humanities methods: open data, OCR, text analysis, and visualization. Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoctoral fellow at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid, with the project [JesLibSouth](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101200600): *Books in the Borderlands of the Iberian Worlds: Jesuit Libraries and Cultural Transformation in South America (17th and 18th Centuries)*.

## Projects

- **[The libraries of the Jesuit missions in Spanish South America (1767–1768)](https://vegafabianr.github.io/jesuit-missionary-libraries/).** Interactive visualization of the library inventories of 74 missions, with the full transcription and open data. [Repository](https://github.com/vegafabianr/jesuit-missionary-libraries)
- **[A corpus of early modern Catholic texts in Spanish](https://github.com/vegafabianr/catholic-pastoral-texts).** 151 texts, about 17 million words, in a clean and a modernized version, with a [cartography of what their passages do](https://vegafabianr.github.io/catholic-pastoral-texts/cartography/).
